Uzbekistan National Sports & Games

From ancient horse disciplines to wrestling, explore the true spirit of Uzbek heritage.

Kupkari (Ulak)

This ancient equestrian sport requires immense bravery and skill. Riders battle on horseback to carry a goat carcass to the target area, proving their ultimate strength and stamina.

Kurash Wrestling

With a history dating back 3,500 years, Kurash is a traditional form of upright wrestling. It is a sport of pure technique and respect, where striking or ground fighting is strictly forbidden.
